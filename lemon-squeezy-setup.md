### Short Description

### Full Description

**See your feed before you post.**

Stop guessing how your Instagram grid will look. This Notion widget lets you:

- **Preview your grid** — See exactly how posts will appear together
- **Drag to rearrange** — Perfect your feed aesthetic before posting
- **Track post status** — Idea → Draft → Ready → Scheduled → Live
- **Export as PNG** — Share your planned grid with clients or team

**How it works:**
1. Duplicate the Notion template to your workspace
2. Add your posts (images, captions, publish dates)
3. Embed the widget to see your visual grid
4. Drag posts to rearrange until it looks perfect

Works with any Notion workspace. No coding required.
